CricStreamr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

CricStreamr is operated by Avondale Heights Cricket Club (AHCC), Melbourne, Australia. This policy explains what the app does with information.

What the app accesses

  • Camera and microphone — used only to capture the live video and audio you choose to stream.
  • Photos — used only to pick sponsor logos and, if enabled, to save highlight clips to your library.
  • YouTube account (optional) — if you sign in with Google, the app uses the YouTube Data API to create, start and end live broadcasts and upload highlight clips to your channel. The sign-in token is stored in your device's Keychain.
  • Match data — public scores are fetched from Play Cricket to draw the scoreboard.

What we collect

CricStreamr does not collect, sell or share personal information. The app has no analytics or advertising. Settings stay on your device. If you configure a highlights server, the app sends match events (fours, sixes, wickets, timestamps and team names) to that server so clips can be cut.

Google user data

CricStreamr's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to manage your own broadcasts and uploads. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by signing out in the app. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Video content

Streams and clips are published to YouTube according to the privacy setting you choose (public, unlisted or private). To request removal of footage that includes you, contact us below.
