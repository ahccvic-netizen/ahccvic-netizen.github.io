CricStreamr

Live cricket streaming for Avondale Heights Cricket Club

What CricStreamr is

CricStreamr is an iPhone app made by Avondale Heights Cricket Club (AHCC) in Melbourne, Australia. Club volunteers use it to film local club cricket matches and broadcast them live on YouTube so players, families and supporters can watch from anywhere.

Features

  • Live streaming to YouTube from the iPhone camera and microphone, in 720p or 1080p.
  • Live scoreboard overlay showing score, overs, batters, bowler and the current over, using public match data from Play Cricket.
  • One-tap Go Live — the app creates the YouTube live broadcast, starts it, and ends it when the match finishes.
  • Sponsor logos displayed on the stream for club sponsors.
  • Highlights — fours, sixes and wickets are logged as YouTube chapters and can be saved as short highlight clips.

How CricStreamr uses Google user data

CricStreamr asks for access to a YouTube account only so it can broadcast and publish the club's match videos on that account. It requests:

PermissionWhy it is needed
Manage your YouTube account
youtube		To create a live broadcast and stream, start the broadcast, set its privacy (public, unlisted or private), and end it when the match is over.
Upload YouTube videos
youtube.upload		To upload short highlight clips (fours, sixes, wickets) from the match to the same channel.

CricStreamr does not read, collect, store on any server, sell or share any other YouTube or Google account data. Sign-in tokens are kept only in the iPhone's secure Keychain. Access can be removed at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions or by signing out in the app.

Who can use it

CricStreamr is used by AHCC volunteers to stream to the club's YouTube channel. A volunteer may optionally sign in with their own Google account to stream to their own channel instead.
