Features

Everything a club needs to broadcast a match

Built by volunteers who stream every weekend, for the way club cricket actually works.

Go live in one tap

CricStreamr creates the YouTube broadcast for you, starts it, and ends it cleanly when you stop.

  • Public, unlisted or private — your choice per team
  • Stream to the club channel or your own
  • Live chat link right on screen

A live scoreboard on every stream

The overlay updates automatically from Play Cricket, so viewers always know the state of the game.

  • Score, overs, run rate and target
  • Batters on strike, bowler figures and the current over
  • Your team names and club colours
● LIVE

Every four, six and wicket marked

Big moments are logged as they happen and turned into YouTube chapters, so players and families can jump straight to them.

  • One tap to copy chapters into YouTube
  • Optional highlight clips
📶

Built for weak signal

Quality adjusts automatically when mobile data drops, and the stream reconnects on its own.

⏸

Pause for breaks

🏷️

Sponsor logos

HD video

Any format

Ready before the first ball

Add your team

Find the match

Tap Go Live

Mount the phone, frame the pitch and you're broadcasting.
